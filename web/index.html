X and Y are independent hardware groups; both can be held with A/B/Start. Portrait cartridges rotate the screen counterclockwise. Key and touch labels always select the same raw buttons; they do not rotate with the image. Beacon Trail uses the X group.

Click the screen to use the keyboard. Touch controls appear on small or touch screens. Hiding the tab pauses; choose Resume when you return.

Changing the console restarts the current cartridge. Local ROM files stay in your browser and are never uploaded.

Compatibility

The original demo is silent and uses monochrome tiles on all three models. The core also supports color layers/sprites, cartridge saves, interrupts/timers, four-channel sound, and Sound DMA/Hyper Voice PCM on Color and Crystal. Compatibility is limited; unsupported instructions or hardware operations stop execution. Saves stay in this browser, keyed by ROM content.

Sound

Sound defaults to on at 70% after you choose Play demo or Load ROM. Browsers may require another click or tap on Enable audio to allow playback. Mute / Unmute keeps your volume level; the 0–100% slider adjusts output live (0% is silent). Raising the slider while muted keeps it muted. Sound preferences stay in this browser, separately from cartridge saves. Unavailable audio does not stop gameplay. Beacon Trail is deliberately silent.
