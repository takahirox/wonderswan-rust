Click the screen to use the keyboard. Hold X and Y directions together with A, B, or Start. Portrait cartridges rotate the picture counterclockwise and keep the same button assignments.

Touch controls appear on small or touch screens. Hiding the tab pauses gameplay. Choose Resume when you return.

Display

Retro CRT adds a mask and scanlines. Clean shows sharp pixels. LCD Light adds subtle cells and persistence. LCD Authentic uses a stronger effect tailored to each console. Your choice stays in this browser. If graphics effects fail, the player uses Clean.

After choosing a display effect, press Escape or click the game screen to return to gameplay input.

Cartridge settings

Changing Console restarts the loaded cartridge.

Compatibility

Unsupported instructions or hardware operations can stop gameplay. Cartridge saves stay in this browser and follow the ROM content.

Sound

Sound starts at 70% by default. If the browser blocks playback, choose Enable audio. Mute preserves your volume level. Sound settings stay in this browser.
