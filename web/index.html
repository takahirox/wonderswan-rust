X and Y are independent hardware groups; both can be held with A/B/Start. Portrait cartridges rotate the screen counterclockwise. Key and touch labels always select the same raw buttons; they do not rotate with the image.

Click the screen to use the keyboard. Touch controls appear on small or touch screens. Hiding the tab pauses; choose Resume when you return.

Changing the console restarts the current cartridge. Local ROM files stay in your browser and are never uploaded.

Retro CRT (default) adds a stylistic mask and scanlines; the WonderSwan had an LCD, not a CRT. Clean keeps exact, sharp pixels. LCD Light adds subtle cells and persistence; LCD Authentic strengthens them with modest mono, Color and SwanCrystal panel approximations, not calibrated measurements. These optional effects only change presentation. Your choice stays in this browser; if graphics effects fail, Clean takes over.

Compatibility

The core supports color layers/sprites, cartridge saves, interrupts/timers, four-channel sound, and Sound DMA/Hyper Voice PCM on Color and Crystal. Compatibility is limited; unsupported instructions or hardware operations stop execution. Saves stay in this browser, keyed by ROM content.

Sound

Sound defaults to on at 70% after you choose Load ROM. Browsers may require another click or tap on Enable audio to allow playback. Mute / Unmute keeps your volume level; the 0–100% slider adjusts output live (0% is silent). Raising the slider while muted keeps it muted. Sound preferences stay in this browser, separately from cartridge saves. Unavailable audio does not stop gameplay.
